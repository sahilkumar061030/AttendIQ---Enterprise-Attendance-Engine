## Looked at, NOT a defect

1. **`date` and shift times stored as plain strings (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `HH:MM`)**: While it may look tempting to store them as datetimes, `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly specifies calendar dates and shift times as plain strings to avoid timezone drift.
2. **Excluding `_id` from API requests and responses**: `_id` is an internal MongoDB ObjectId. `openapi.yaml` explicitly dictates that employees are identified by `emp_code` and attendance records by `(emp_code, date)`. Keeping `_id` hidden is intentional.
3. **Handling legacy records with missing `history` or `half_day`**: `DATA_MODEL.md` document 9 explicitly states that older records may omit `history` or `half_day`. Treating missing values as `[]` and `False` is expected behavior, not missing data error handling.
4. **`present_days` counting only Mon-Fri while weekend logs count for late and overtime**: While seemingly asymmetrical, Rule R7 explicitly mandates that weekend logs contribute to overtime and late totals, but only Monday-Friday records contribute to `present_days`.
5. **Null `attendance_rate` on weekends and zero-headcount days in trend**: Trend rows on non-working days or days with zero headcount having `null` attendance rate is explicitly defined by the API contract.
